  The Beats Flex Wireless Earphones remain Apple's most affordable and practical entry-level audio option. Because they use a flexible neckband design rather than a true wireless form factor, they offer exceptional battery life and a secure fit for daily casual listening. 
  Deep-Dive Technical Specifications
  Audio & Acoustic Hardware
  
      Drivers: Proprietary dual-element acoustic drivers with layered drivers for clear sound and deep bass.
      Microphone: Built-in microphone with wind-reduction technology to enhance voice clarity during calls.
      Acoustic Design: Laser-cut micro-venting ports provide ear pressure relief while keeping the bass response punchy. 
  
  Connectivity & Processing Power
  
      Core Processor: Integrated Apple W1 Chip, enabling Class 1 Bluetooth connectivity for extended range.
      Ecosystem Sync: Supports one-touch proximity pairing and instant switching across your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.
      Audio Sharing: Lets you split audio wirelessly with any other pair of Beats headphones or Apple AirPods nearby.
      Android Compatibility: Fully compatible with Android via the Beats app for battery tracking, widget support, and firmware updates. 
  
  Power & Charging Specs
  
      Total Playback Time: Up to 12 hours of continuous audio playback on a single full charge.
      Fast Fuel Technology: A quick 10-minute charge via the USB-C port provides up to 1.5 hours of playback when the battery is low.
      Port Type: Native USB-C charging interface (includes a short USB-C to USB-C charging cable in the box). 
  
  Physical Design & Dimensions
  
      Cable Material: Crafted from a highly flexible, durable Nitinol (nickel-titanium) shape-memory alloy cable.
      Weight: Extremely lightweight at only 0.66 ounces (18.6 grams).
      Ear Tips: Ships with four size options (Small, Medium, Large, and Dual-Flange) to ensure an airtight acoustic seal.
