  The Xiaomi 15T 5G (Global Version)
  is a high-performance, GSM factory-unlocked smartphone released in September 2025. This international model is designed for global compatibility and is compatible with GSM carriers such as T-Mobile, Mint Mobile, and Tello in the U.S., but it does not work with CDMA networks like Verizon or AT&T. 
  Key Specifications
  
      Performance: Driven by the MediaTek Dimensity 8400-Ultra 4nm chipset with a peak clock speed of 3.25 GHz.
      Display: Features a 6.83-inch AMOLED "eye-care" flat display with a 1.5K resolution (2772 x 1280), 120Hz refresh rate, and a peak brightness of 3,200 nits.
      Leica Camera System: A triple rear setup co-engineered with Leica, featuring a 50MP main (Light Fusion 800) sensor, 50MP telephoto (2x optical zoom), and 12MP ultra-wide lens.
      Battery & Charging: Equipped with a large 5,500 mAh battery supporting 67W wired fast charging, reaching a full charge in approximately 50-51 minutes.
      Software: Runs on Xiaomi HyperOS 2 based on Android 15, with a promise of four major OS updates and six years of security patches.
      Durability: Holds an IP68 rating for water and dust resistance and is protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 7i.
