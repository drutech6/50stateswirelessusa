  (Phone not Included) The Cricket Wireless Select Unlimited™ Plan provides unlimited high-speed data, talk, and text utilizing AT&T's nationwide 5G network. It is designed as an entry-level unlimited option that balances essential network access with a lower price point by excluding premium add-ons.  Primary Plan Features
  
      Unlimited Data: Access to 5G network speeds, though Cricket may temporarily slow down data speeds during heavy cellular network congestion.
      Video Quality: Video streaming is restricted to Standard Definition (SD) at roughly 480p.
      North American Calling: Includes unlimited voice calls and text messages originating from the U.S. to destinations in Mexico and Canada. 
  
  Key Restrictions & Exclusions
  
      Availability Restriction: This plan is explicitly reserved for new lines of service only (via a new number or port-in) and is unavailable to existing customers trying to switch tiers.
      No Built-In Hotspot: Mobile hotspot capabilities are omitted, though a 10GB hotspot add-on can be manually attached for an extra $10 per month.
      No International Roaming: Cellular data usage and roaming inside Mexico or Canada are completely blocked.
      No Global Texting: International texting to countries outside of North America is unavailable.
      No Entertainment Add-ons: Max (HBO Max with Ads) and cloud storage tiers are entirely excluded.
