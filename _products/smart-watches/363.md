  The Fitbit Versa 4 builds on the brand’s strong fitness tracking heritage by offering a sleek, lightweight design with core smartwatch functionality. In terms of appearance, the Versa 4 features an aluminum case paired with silicone bands, and is available in multiple finishes—Black/Graphite Aluminum, Pink Sand/Copper Rose Aluminum, and Waterfall Blue/Platinum Aluminum—so you can choose a style to match your preference. Its form-factor is comfortable for all-day wear, whether you’re working out, going to the office, or winding down for sleep.
  
  On the feature side, the Versa 4 offers built-in GPS for outdoor workouts, 24/7 heart-rate monitoring, sleep tracking, and over 40 exercise modes to capture a wide variety of activities. It also provides the “Daily Readiness” score, which helps indicate whether your body is ready for an intense workout or if you should lean toward recovery. Smart features include notifications from your phone for calls and texts, app alerts, and support for Fitbit Pay and Google Wallet (depending on region).
  
  In terms of durability and daily wear-capability, the Versa 4 is water resistant to 50 m (5 ATM), making it suitable for swimming or showering though not for deep-water diving. The display is a bright AMOLED touchscreen that remains readable in a variety of lighting conditions, and the overall construction is well-suited for active use.
  
  Overall, the Fitbit Versa 4 offers a balanced blend of fitness tracking depth, reasonable smart features, and wearable comfort—with a design that fits both workout and everyday scenarios.
