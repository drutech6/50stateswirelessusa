  The Coby CHBT835
  (often cataloged as the Wireless Metal Folding Headphones) is a budget-friendly, ultra-portable over-ear headset. It stands out in the entry-level market by swapping out the all-plastic frames typical of its price point for a more durable, metallic architectural layout. 
  Core Technical Specifications
  
      Acoustic Hardware: Driven by standard 40mm speaker drivers. They are tuned with a standard 20Hz–20kHz frequency response, 32 Ohm impedance, and 100±3 dB sensitivity to provide a clear, balanced every-day listening sound profile. 
      Battery & Power: A full 2-hour charge cycle yields up to 10 hours of continuous wireless playback. 
      Wireless Range: Employs Bluetooth connectivity providing a stable wireless operation radius of 33 to 49 feet depending on the specific firmware version batch. 
      Standalone Playback: Includes an integrated TF/MicroSD card slot alongside a USB port, allowing you to load MP3s and stream music directly from the headset without being connected to a smartphone or computer. 
  
  Design & Architecture
  
      Reinforced Metal Frame: Built with a folding metal chassis and an adjustable padded headband, allowing the ear cups to collapse inward for compact storage during travel. 
      On-Ear Tactile Controls: Outfitted with physical navigation buttons directly on the ear cup shell. These allow you to skip tracks, adjust the volume, play/pause media, and manage phone calls without touching your source hardware. 
      Backup Wired Connection: Features a built-in 3.5mm AUX input port. If your battery dies on the move, you can plug in an auxiliary cable to seamlessly switch to passive, zero-latency wired audio.
