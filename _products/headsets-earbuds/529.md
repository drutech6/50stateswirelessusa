  The technical specifications for the Samsung Galaxy Buds 3 Pro highlight their high-fidelity hardware, upgraded Bluetooth standard, and enhanced durability rating:
  Audio Architecture
  
      Drivers: Custom dual-driver system (10mm dynamic woofer + 6.1mm planar tweeter).
      Amplification: Independent dual-amplifiers separating low and high frequencies.
      Audio Quality: Up to 24-bit / 96kHz High-Resolution wireless streaming.
      Supported Codecs: Samsung Seamless Codec (SSC) Hi-Fi, AAC, SBC.
  
  Connectivity & Control
  
      Bluetooth Version: Bluetooth 5.4.
      Multipoint: Seamless auto-switch capability (limited to Samsung Galaxy devices running One UI 4.1.1 or later).
      Physical Interface: Pinch-and-squeeze stem sensors for playback; swipe up/down for hardware volume control.
      Visual Elements: Integrated LED "Blade Lights" on the stems to indicate status.
  
  Battery & Charging
  
      Earbud Battery Capacity: 53 mAh (each).
      Case Battery Capacity: 515 mAh.
      Playtime (ANC On): Up to 6 hours continuous (26 hours total with charging case).
      Playtime (ANC Off): Up to 7 hours continuous (30 hours total with charging case).
      Talk Time (ANC On): Up to 4.5 hours continuous (20 hours total with charging case).
      Charging Methods: USB-C wired connection or Qi-certified wireless charging pads.
  
  Dimensions & Durability
  
      Earbud Dimensions: 18.1 x 19.8 x 33.2 mm.
      Earbud Weight: 5.4 grams per earbud.
      Case Dimensions: 48.7 x 58.9 x 24.4 mm.
      Case Weight: 46.5 grams.
      Ingress Protection: IP57 rated (dust-protected and fresh water submersion up to 1 meter for 30 minutes. Wireless Connectivity & Transmission
  
      Bluetooth Version: Bluetooth 5.4.
      Effective Range: Up to 30 feet (10 meters) under clear, unobstructed conditions.
      Transmission Profiles: Supports A2DP, AVRCP, and HFP wireless transmission protocols.
  
  Supported Wireless Codecs
  
      Samsung Seamless Codec (SSC): Streams proprietary 24-bit / 96kHz ultra-high-quality audio to compatible Samsung Galaxy devices.
      AAC: Standard high-quality streaming codec optimized for iOS hardware.
      SBC: Universal fallback audio codec compatible with all modern Bluetooth sources.
      LE Audio (LC3): Supported out of the box for lower power consumption and improved transmission efficiency over compatible hardware.
