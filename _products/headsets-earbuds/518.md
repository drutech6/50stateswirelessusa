description: |
  The Apple AirPods 4 (2024) represents the standard baseline edition of Apple's 4th generation open-ear wireless earbuds. This model does not feature Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), making it the direct successor to the AirPods 2 and AirPods 3 for users who want premium Apple ecosystem features at a lower price point. 
  Key Specifications & Features
  
      Apple H2 Chip: Powered by the same advanced processor found in the ANC version and AirPods Pro 2. It enables computational audio upgrades like Voice Isolation to strip out background noise during phone calls.
      Redesigned Fit: Built with an updated, ergonomically mapped shape engineered to comfortably fit a wider variety of ear types. The stem is shorter and retains the pressure-sensitive force sensor for media controls.
      Siri Gestures: Supports Siri Interactions, allowing you to nod your head "yes" or shake your head "no" to answer or decline incoming calls and notifications hands-free.
      Personalized Spatial Audio: Includes internal gyroscopes and accelerometers to deliver immersive 3D audio with dynamic head tracking for movies, music, and games.
      IP54 Durability: Both the earbuds and the charging case are dust, sweat, and water-resistant, making them fully safe for gym workouts and running. 
  
  Battery & Charging Logistics
  
      Playtime: Delivers up to 5 hours of continuous listening time on a single charge.
      Total Reservoir: The charging case provides up to 30 hours of total listening time.
      Fast Fuel: A quick 5-minute dock inside the case yields roughly 1 hour of playback.
      Charging Limitations: The standard case charges strictly via a USB-C cable. Unlike the upgraded ANC model's case, this baseline case does not support wireless charging (Qi or Apple Watch) and lacks the built-in speaker for Find My proximity tracking.
