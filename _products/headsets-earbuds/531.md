  The Samsung Galaxy Buds Core (SM-R410)
  are Samsung's ultra-budget entry in their true wireless lineup, focusing on flagship-style smart features at an accessible entry price, they provide a highly competitive option that sits below the Galaxy Buds FE. 
  Audio Design & Codecs
  
      Single Dynamic Driver: Houses a single custom dynamic driver tuned primarily for deep bass and clear, prominent vocal frequencies.
      Acoustic Profile: Delivers a consumer-friendly, bass-forward sound signature that handles everyday streaming smoothly.
      Samsung Scalable Codec (SSC): Utilizes Samsung's proprietary high-efficiency audio codec alongside baseline AAC and SBC profiles. SSC automatically adjusts streaming bitrates to prevent dropouts on paired Galaxy devices. 
  
  Noise Management & Microphones
  
      Adaptive Active Noise Cancellation: Employs effective ANC filters to cut low-frequency environmental rumble like engine noise or city office hums.
      Ambient Transparency Mode: Pipes outside environmental audio through the earbud array so you can retain quick situational awareness.
      Six-Microphone System: Uses three integrated mics per earbud to balance voice isolation, background environmental noise reduction, and ongoing ANC processing. 
  
  Ecosystem Integration & AI
  
      Galaxy AI Compatibility: Leverages native device processing to stream face-to-face translations using Galaxy AI Interpreter Mode directly to your ears.
      Smart Cross-Device Switch: Uses the Samsung cloud matrix to hot-swap audio feeds between your Galaxy smartphone, tablet, or Galaxy Watch automatically depending on active feeds.
      Samsung Find Mesh: Coordinates with the global tracking network, enabling your buds to ring aloud for easy tracking if misplaced. 
  
  Hardware, Shell, & Battery
  
      Secure Wingtip Ergonomics: Swaps the stem look for a streamlined, rounded pill design complete with stabilization wings for pressure-free workout tracking.
      IP54 Rugged Shielding: Provides a certified protective barrier against fine dust and incoming splashes from all directions.
      35-Hour Total Playtime: The buds run between 6 to 8 hours continuously per charge with ANC active. The compact clamshell case pushes total playback limits to 35 hours when ANC is turned off.
