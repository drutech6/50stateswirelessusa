  The Bose QuietComfort Earbuds II
  are premium true wireless earbuds celebrated for delivering some of the world's best active noise cancellation (ANC). Launched at an original retail price of $279.99, they feature a smaller, more streamlined design than their predecessors and utilize custom sound calibration. 
  Industry-Leading Noise Cancellation & Audio
  
      CustomTune Technology: Every time you put the earbuds in, they play a chime to map the unique shape of your ear canal, instantly optimizing both the audio frequency and the ANC performance.
      Aware Mode with ActiveSense: Pipes in ambient sound so you can hear your surroundings, while automatically dampening sudden loud background noises (like a passing siren or construction) to a comfortable level.
      Bose Signature Sound: Delivers deep bass and balanced high-fidelity audio, which can be further adjusted using the custom EQ presets within the [Bose Music App](1.1.8, 1.4.3). 
  
  Hardware, Fit, & Battery
  
      Bose Fit Kit: Replaces traditional one-piece ear tips with a two-piece system, combining three sizes of umbrella-shaped silicone tips with three separate stability bands to ensure a secure, customized seal.
      Battery Life: Provides up to 6 hours of continuous runtime with ANC turned on, plus an additional 18 hours (three full charges) from the case. A quick 20-minute charge yields up to 2 hours of playback.
      Durability: Features an IPX4 rating, meaning the earbuds easily withstand sweat and rain during workouts or outdoor runs. 
  
  Missing Features & Considerations
  
      No Wireless Charging: Unlike competing premium models, the stock charging case must be plugged in via USB-C unless you purchase a separate Qi-compatible silicone sleeve accessory.
      No Bluetooth Multipoint: These earbuds lack the ability to connect to two devices simultaneously (like a laptop and phone) right out of the box.
