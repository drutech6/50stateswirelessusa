  The i8 Pro Max
  is a budget-friendly smartwatch designed to mimic the appearance of the Apple Watch Series 8. It is primarily marketed for its calling capabilities and fitness tracking features. 
  Core Specifications
  
      Display: Features a 1.75-inch to 2-inch IPS HD touchscreen with a resolution typically listed between 320 x 380 pixels and 325 x 380 pixels.
      Build: Constructed with an alloy case and a silicone strap. It has a 44mm surface diameter.
      Water Resistance: Rated as IP67 or IP68. While some sources claim IP68, retailers often warn against swimming or diving with the device.
      Connectivity: Uses Bluetooth 4.2 or 5.0 to sync with smartphones. It is compatible with Android 5.0+ and iOS 9.0+. 
  
  Key Features
  
      Bluetooth Calling: Built-in microphone and loudspeaker allow you to answer and dial calls directly from the watch.
      Health Monitoring: Includes sensors for heart rate tracking, blood oxygen (SpO2), blood pressure, and sleep monitoring.
      Fitness Tracking: Tracks steps, calories burned, and distance for activities like walking, running, and cycling.
      Smart Notifications: Syncs alerts for WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter, and SMS.
      Utility Tools: Features include a remote camera shutter ("remote selfie"), music control, alarm clock, stopwatch, and find-your-phone function. 
  
  Battery and Charging
  
      Capacity: Models vary between 220mAh, 280mAh, and 380mAh.
      Battery Life: Typically offers 1–2 days of active use and up to 3–7 days on standby.
      Charging: Uses a magnetic charger.
