  The JBL Charge 6
  is a portable, rugged Bluetooth speaker released in early 2025. It builds on the popular Charge 5
  by introducing several key upgrades, including Auracast™ for multi-speaker pairing, a detachable carry strap, and an improved IP68 waterproof and dustproof rating that now includes 1-meter drop protection. 
  Key Specifications
  
      Audio Performance: Delivers 45W of peak power (30W RMS woofer + 15W RMS tweeter) using a two-way driver system and AI Sound Boost to minimize distortion at high volumes.
      Battery Life: Offers up to 24 hours of standard playtime, which can be extended to 28 hours using the "Playtime Boost" feature in the JBL Portable app.
      Charging: Features a 3-hour full charge time via USB-C and a quick-charge function providing 2.5 hours of playback from just 10 minutes of charging.
      Connectivity: Uses Bluetooth 5.4 for stable wireless streaming and supports lossless audio playback when connected via its USB-C port to a compatible source. 
  
  Notable Features
  
      Built-in Powerbank: True to the "Charge" name, it can use its battery to charge smartphones and other mobile devices via its USB-C port.
      Sturdy Design: Features a redesigned, flatter base for better stability and beefed-up rubber bumpers on the passive radiators for enhanced durability.
      Advanced Customization: The companion app now offers a 7-band equalizer, a significant upgrade over the 3-band EQ found on the previous model.
