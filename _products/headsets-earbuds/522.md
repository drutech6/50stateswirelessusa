  The Apple AirPods (3rd Gen) with Lightning Charging Case (2021)
  represents the wired-only configuration of Apple's redesigned third-generation open-ear earbuds. This specific model completely swaps out wireless charging compatibility to offer a more budget-friendly price point while retaining the identical acoustic hardware and performance of the standard 3rd Gen lineup. 
  Key Performance Specs & Features
  
      Wired-Only Charging Case: The bundled charging case (Model A2897) can only be charged using a physical Lightning cable connected to a USB-C or USB-A wall brick. It lacks internal magnetic induction coils, meaning it will not react if placed on a MagSafe magnet, a standard Qi wireless pad, or an Apple Watch puck.
      Personalized Spatial Audio: Uses advanced gyroscopes and accelerometers to deliver multi-dimensional 3D audio with dynamic head tracking, positioning theater-like sound fields all around you when streaming compatible Dolby Atmos media.
      Apple H1 Processor: Driven by the custom H1 chip, providing instantaneous proximity pairing, automatic shifting between your iCloud-linked devices, and hands-free "Hey Siri" tracking.
      Contoured Open Fit: Designed with an ergonomically mapped contour that sits snugly at an optimal acoustic angle without using deep, rubberized silicone ear tips. The stems feature physical pressure-sensitive force sensors to let you effortlessly squeeze to control playback or phone calls.
      IPX4 Ruggedness: Both the earbuds and the Lightning case are certified IPX4 sweat and water-resistant, making them safe against rain or heavy workouts. 
  
  Battery Performance
  
      Earbud Playback: Delivers up to 6 hours of continuous listening or up to 4 hours of continuous talk time on a single full charge.
      Total Runtime Reservoir: Tucking the earbuds back into the case expands total mobile operational life to 30 hours before you need to plug in a Lightning cable.
      Fast Fuel: A rapid 5-minute dock inside the charging case yields 1 hour of continuous music playback when your batteries drop to zero.
