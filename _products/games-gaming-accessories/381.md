  The Meta Quest 3S is the budget-friendly alternative to the Quest 3, featuring the same powerful Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2 chipset but using older Fresnel lenses to lower the cost. Core Technical Specifications The Quest 3S matches the Quest 3's performance and mixed reality capabilities but utilizes the display and lens stack from the Quest 2 : Performance & Power: Processor: Qualcomm Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2, providing double the GPU power of the Quest 2 . Memory: 8GB RAM (33% more than the Quest 2 ). Storage: Available in 128GB and 256GB configurations. Display & Optics: Resolution: 1832 x 1920 pixels per eye. Lenses: Fresnel lenses with a 3-position inter-axial distance (IAD) adjustment (58mm, 63mm, and 68mm). Refresh Rate: Supports 72Hz, 90Hz, and 120Hz. Field of View: 96° horizontal and 90° vertical. Mixed Reality & Tracking: Passthrough: Dual 4MP RGB color cameras (18 PPD resolution) for high-fidelity full-color views of your surroundings. Sensors: 4 IR cameras for head and hand tracking, plus 2 flood LEDs for improved low-light hand tracking. Depth Sensing: Uses software-based depth estimation rather than the physical depth sensor found on the Quest 3. Audio & Design: Audio: Integrated stereo speakers with spatial audio. Note that the Quest 3S lacks a 3.5mm headphone jack, requiring USB-C or Bluetooth headphones. Weight: 514 grams. Battery: 4,324 mAh battery, lasting approximately 2.5 hours on average—slightly longer than the Quest 3 due to its lower-resolution display. What's in the Box?
