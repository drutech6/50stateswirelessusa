  The JBL Tune 520BT Wireless On-Ear Headphones are a major wireless upgrade to the entry-level series, delivering a massive 57-hour battery life and official app customization at a budget price point. Online audio reviewers praise them as an incredibly long-lasting daily commuter option that packs deep, punchy low-end performance. 
  Core Technical Upgrades & Features
  
      Up to 57 Hours of Power: Features a highly efficient battery offering up to 57 hours of straight playback. Full recharging takes exactly 2 hours over a Type-C cable.
      Lightning-Fast Speed Charge: Connecting the headset to a power cable for just 5 minutes provides 3 full hours of instant wireless playback.
      Modern Bluetooth 5.3: Upgraded wireless protocols ensure lower power draw, faster pairing, and extended line-of-sight range.
      Dual-Device Multipoint Connection: Seamlessly pairs with two source devices at once. It automatically shifts audio from a movie on your laptop to an incoming call on your phone.
      JBL Headphones App Support: Grants access to a full smartphone software suite. This lets you build custom EQ profiles, alter automated power-off intervals, and update firmware.
      Hands-Free Voice Aware: The integrated microphone setup lets you balance exactly how much of your own voice you can hear echoing back through the drivers during a live phone call.
      33mm Dynamic Drivers: Imbued with signature JBL Pure Bass sound parameters to maximize lower-frequency tracking and high output volumes. 
  
  Upgrading from the Wired Tune 500
  If you are moving up from the wired JBL Tune 500 headset you checked out previously, the 520BT
  model introduces drastic daily enhancements:
  
      Wireless Freedom: Replaces the permanent 46-inch audio ribbon wire entirely with cord-free Bluetooth tracking. Note that the 520BT
      is strictly a wireless headset and does not feature a backup 3.5mm jack.
      Custom Sound Tuning: The Tune 500
      relies on static, hard-coded factory settings. The Tune 520BT
      links to a full graphical EQ application to fine-tune audio bands to your exact preferences.
      Physical Architecture: While the physical on-ear size profile and folding hinge layout are near identical, the 520BT
      moves all media adjustments from an in-line wire button up to tactile on-ear earcup switches
