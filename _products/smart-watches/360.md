  The WITHit Giga Smartwatch presents a large, vibrant 2.04-inch high-definition AMOLED touchscreen—with a resolution of 386 × 448 pixels—that fills its face and offers easily readable visuals and customizable watch faces. The case is aluminum (or ABS/aluminum blend depending on source) and the handset comes with a quick-release silicone band for comfort and interchangeability. Designed for modern wearers who want connected features and style on a budget, the Giga supports both Android 5.0+ and iOS 9.0+ through the WITHit Smartwatch App, enabling smartphone notifications, Bluetooth calling, music controls, and watch face customization.
  
  In terms of health and activity tracking, this smartwatch offers step counting, heart-rate monitoring, blood oxygen (SpO₂) tracking, sleep tracking, and women’s health/menstrual cycle tracking. It also supports general smartwatch features such as incoming call handling, notification alerts for apps like WhatsApp and Snapchat, remote camera control, and music control via the connected phone. Battery life claims run up to approximately 120 hours in light use. On the durability side, it is rated for water resistance up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes—enough for light water exposure—but it is not built for heavy submersion or diving.
  
  While the Giga delivers impressive value, reviewers note some trade-offs: it uses a proprietary charging cable, the screen lacks adaptive brightness (making outdoor visibility and night use less optimal), and some users report inconsistent metrics in activity tracking.
