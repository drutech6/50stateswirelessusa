  The PlayStation Portal Remote Player
  is a dedicated handheld streaming device designed specifically for the PlayStation 5. It features an 8-inch LCD touchscreen that supports 1080p resolution at up to 60 FPS. While it does not have internal storage for local games, a March 2026 update introduced a 1080p High Quality mode, which increases the bitrate for sharper visuals during both Remote Play and cloud streaming. 
  Core Technical Specifications
  Feature 	Specification Details
  Display	8-inch LCD Touchscreen, 1080p (Full HD) resolution
  Performance	Up to 60 FPS
  New 2026 Feature	1080p High Quality Mode (High bitrate for clearer image)
  Controls	Full DualSense features: Haptic feedback, Adaptive triggers
  Connectivity	Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac), USB-C (charging), 3.5mm jack
  Audio	Built-in speakers, PlayStation Link for ultra-low latency audio
  Battery Life	Approx. 4–5 hours (highly dependent on brightness/haptics)
  Connectivity & Play Modes
  
      Remote Play: Streams games directly from your PS5
      or PS5 Pro
      over Wi-Fi.
      Cloud Streaming (Premium): PlayStation Plus Premium members can stream select PS5 and classic titles directly from the cloud without a console.
      Network Requirements: Requires at least 5 Mbps for basic use, but 15 Mbps or higher is recommended for the best experience.
      Audio Constraints: Does not support standard Bluetooth audio; you must use wired headphones or PlayStation Link devices like the PULSE Elite or Explore.
