  The Sony PlayStation VR2 (PS VR2) is built with high-end display and sensory technology designed specifically for the PS5.
  Visuals & Display
  
      Display Technology: Dual 4K HDR OLED panels.
      Resolution: 2000 x 2040 per eye (4000 x 2040 combined).
      Refresh Rate: 90Hz and 120Hz modes for smooth motion.
      Field of View (FOV): Approximately 110 degrees.
      Lens Type: Fresnel lenses with an adjustable lens dial (IPD adjustment) to line up the optics with your eyes.
  
  Sensory & Tracking
  
      Eye Tracking: Uses an internal IR camera to track where you are looking, enabling Foveated Rendering (concentrating graphical power exactly where you look).
      Headset Feedback: A built-in motor provides subtle vibrations to the headset for added immersion (e.g., feeling the rush of an object passing your head).
      Tracking Method: Inside-out tracking via four integrated cameras—no external base stations or sensors required.
      Audio: Integrated microphone and a 3.5mm jack for the included stereo headphones or your own headset.
  
  Sense Controllers
  
      Haptics: Advanced haptic feedback and Adaptive Triggers (L2/R2) that provide variable resistance.
      Finger Touch Detection: The controller senses the position of your fingers without pressing a button, allowing for more natural gestures.
      Charging: Internal rechargeable batteries via USB-C.
  
  Connectivity & Weight
  
      Connection: A single USB-C cable (approx. 14.7 feet long) connects directly to the front of the PS5.
      Weight: Approximately 560 grams (excluding the cable), which is lighter and more balanced than the original PS VR.
