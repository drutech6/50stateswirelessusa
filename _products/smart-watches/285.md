  The Apple Watch SE (2nd Generation)
  , released in 2022, is designed as a value-focused smartwatch that provides core Apple Watch features. It uses the S8 SiP (System in Package), which is the same chip found in the Apple Watch Series 8, making it up to 20% faster than the original SE. 
  Core Technical Specifications
  
      Case Sizes: Available in 40mm and 44mm aluminum cases.
      Display: Retina LTPO OLED display with up to 1,000 nits peak brightness and Ion-X front glass.
          44mm: 368 x 448 pixels (977 sq mm display area).
          40mm: 324 x 394 pixels (759 sq mm display area).
      Processor: S8 SiP with 64-bit dual-core processor and 2-core Neural Engine.
      Storage: 32GB capacity.
      Battery Life: Up to 18 hours of normal use; supports wireless charging via magnetic cable.
      Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.3, Wi-Fi 4 (802.11n), and L1 GPS. Cellular models are available with LTE support.
      Durability: Water-resistant up to 50 meters (swimproof). 
  
  Key Features and Sensors
  
      Health Sensors: Second-generation optical heart sensor, compass, and an always-on altimeter.
      Safety Features: Includes Crash Detection, Fall Detection, and Emergency SOS.
      Redesigned Back: Features a new color-matched back case made of a nylon composite material, which reduces the device's overall weight.
      Software: Compatible with the latest watchOS updates, including health insights like sleep stages and irregular heart rhythm notifications.
