description: |
  The JBL Vibe Buds
  (also marketed in some regions as the Wave Buds) are JBL's entry-level, highly popular everyday wireless earbuds. They are designed to deliver rich sound and great software features at a fraction of the cost of premium models. 
  One unique physical quirk of this model is its open-frame charging case, which lacks a flip-top lid. While magnets lock the earbuds tightly in place so they won't fall out, the top remains open to the elements. 
  Key Technical Specifications
  
      Audio Drivers: 8 mm dynamic drivers pumping out JBL Deep Bass Sound, offering a heavy low-end boost that aligns with the Harman curve.
      Battery Performance: Provides up to 32 hours of total battery life (8 hours in the earbuds, and an extra 24 hours stored in the case). A 10-minute USB-C speed charge yields 2 hours of playback.
      Connectivity: Employs Bluetooth 5.2.
      Durability Protection: The earbuds hold an IP54 dust and water resistance rating (making them great for sand and sweat), while the case carries a lighter IPX2 splash resistance rating.
      App Customization: Unlike most budget earbuds, these fully support the JBL Headphones App. You can customize the touch controls, adjust a full graphic equalizer, and set maximum volume limits.
      Microphone Call System: Built with a single mic per earbud utilizing VoiceAware, a feature that allows you to control how much of your own voice echoes back into the buds during a call. 
  
  Smart Ambient (No ANC)
  The base JBL Vibe Buds
  do not have Active Noise Cancellation. However, because the silicone tips create a very tight passive physical seal in your ear canal to block out background noise, JBL has included their Smart Ambient pass-through modes: 
  
      Ambient Aware: Keeps music playing but channels surrounding environmental noises (like traffic) so you don't get hit by surprises.
      TalkThru: Instantly drops your music volume down low and activates external mics so you can converse without removing the buds.
