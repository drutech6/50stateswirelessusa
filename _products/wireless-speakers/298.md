  The JBL Wind 3
  is a portable Bluetooth speaker specifically designed for cyclists and outdoor enthusiasts, featuring a dedicated handlebar mount for secure attachment to bikes or scooters. 
  Key Features and Specifications
  
      Versatile Playback: Unlike many ultra-portable speakers, it includes a built-in FM radio, a micro SD card slot (TF card) for phone-free listening, and a 3.5mm AUX input.
      Durability: It carries an IP67 rating, making it fully waterproof and dustproof for use in all weather conditions.
      Display & Controls: Features a small LED display to show the current radio frequency and battery level. It also has large, tactile buttons on the front for easy operation while riding.
      Battery Life: Offers up to 5 hours of playtime on a single charge.
      Audio Performance: Equipped with a 5W RMS driver and two EQ modes: "Sport" for enhanced clarity outdoors and "Bass" for indoor listening. 
  
  Expert and User Insights
  
      Mounting Concerns: While the bike mount is the speaker's main selling point, many users on JBL.com and Crutchfield have reported that the plastic mounting clip is fragile and prone to breaking during rides.
      Audio Quality: Reviewers generally praise the sound quality for its size, noting it is clear enough for cycling, though the "Sport" mode can lack bass.
      FM Reception: Some users have noted that the internal FM antenna is relatively weak, which may lead to static in areas with poor signal. 
  
  JBL Wind 3 vs. Wind 3S (Slim)
  There are two versions in this series. The standard Wind 3 includes the FM radio, LED display, and micro SD slot, whereas the Wind 3S
  (Slim) is a Bluetooth-only model without those extra playback features.
