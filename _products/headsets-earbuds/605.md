  The Samsung Galaxy Buds4 are Samsung's latest standard-tier wireless earbuds. They feature an unsealed, open-ear design without silicone tips, catering to users who dislike the "plugged" feeling of traditional in-ear models. They borrow the sleek, flat-faced brushed metal stem design from the Pro line, abandoning the plastic curves of older generations.Key Features & Audio UpgradesPremium Acoustic Architecture: Driven by an updated 11mm dynamic speaker that produces a deep, consumer-friendly bass response and highly articulate treble.Hi-Res Audio Codec: Utilizes the Samsung Seamless Codec to transmit 24-bit / 96kHz near-lossless audio over Bluetooth 6.1.Adaptive ANC 1.0: Employs automated pre-trained algorithms to adjust cancellation levels dynamically, though isolation is lighter than the Pro model due to the open-ear nature.Enhanced Call Quality: Integrates six voice-isolating microphones with Deep Neural Network (DNN) tech and Super Wideband (SWB) processing to deliver clear phone conversations.Hands-Free Head Gestures: Built-in motion tracking allows you to control notifications, as well as nod to accept or shake your head to decline incoming phone calls.Ecosystem AI Features: Deeply integrates with Galaxy devices to enable real-time Live Translate and on-demand language interpreter services.Hardware & Battery SpecsDurability: Rated at IP54 dust and water splash resistance, making them safe against sweat and light rain.Battery Life: Yields up to 5 hours of runtime with ANC turned on, and stretches to 6 hours with ANC disabled.Charging Case: A flat clamshell case with a transparent lid that extends total portable playtime up to 30 hours.What Reviewers and Testers SayVideo reviewers note that while the audio separation is excellent, the unsealed design relies entirely on individual ear anatomy. If your ears match the shape, they remain highly comfortable; otherwise, they can wiggle or slip with intensive movement. Tech testers emphasize that missing out on a tight seal reduces bass sub-frequencies and yields roughly 35% average ambient attenuation compared to the deep silence of the Pro variant
