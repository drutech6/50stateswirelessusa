  The Nintendo Switch Lite

  Feature 	Details
  Display	5.5-inch LCD touchscreen with 1280 x 720 resolution
  Processor	NVIDIA Custom Tegra processor
  Storage	32 GB internal; expandable up to 2TB via microSDHC/microSDXC cards
  RAM	4 GB
  Battery Life	Approximately 3 to 7 hours, depending on usage (e.g., ~4 hours for Breath of the Wild)
  Weight	Approximately 0.61 lbs (275 grams)
  Dimensions	3.6" H x 8.2" W x 0.55" D
  Connectivity	Wi-Fi (802.11 a/b/g/n/ac), Bluetooth 4.1, NFC, and USB-C (for charging only)
  Key Hardware Features
  
      Integrated Controls: The Joy-Con controllers are fused to the system and cannot be detached.
      Handheld Only: Unlike other models, it cannot be docked to a TV and lacks video output hardware.
      Audio: Features built-in stereo speakers and a 3.5mm headphone jack.
      Sensors: Equipped with an accelerometer and a gyroscope for motion control in compatible games.
      Omissions: To maintain its low price and size, it does not include HD Rumble or the IR Motion Camera found on standard Joy-Cons.
