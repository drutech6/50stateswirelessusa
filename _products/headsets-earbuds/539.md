  The JBL Endurance Peak 3
  is a direct evolution of the Peak 2, successfully resolving almost every major limitation of the previous generation while keeping the unshakeable over-ear hook form factor. It is widely considered one of the best heavy-duty athletic earbuds on the market. 
  Key Upgrades Over the Peak 2
  
      Full App & EQ Support: Unlike the Peak 2
      , the Peak 3
      fully connects to the official JBL Headphones App. This unlocks custom equalizer curves, firmware updates, and gesture mapping.
      Ambient Aware & TalkThru: It fixes the lack of transparency options by adding external software mic passthrough. You can safely hear cross-traffic or tap the bud to chat without unhooking them from your ears.
      Massive 50-Hour Battery: Earbud single-charge playtime jumps from 6 hours up to 10 hours continuous playback. Combined with the charging case, total battery capacity reaches 50 hours.
      IP68 Ruggedization Upgrade: Upgraded from IPX7 to an IP68 dust and waterproof rating. The buds are completely sealed against sand or fine dust trails and can survive salt or fresh water submersion up to 1.5 meters deep for 30 minutes.
      Upgraded Calling: Built with a 4-microphone array (2 per side) using dual beamforming technology to better filter out wind roar and ambient gym background noise. 
  
  Core Technical Specifications
  
      Driver Size: 10 mm Dynamic Drivers pushing punchy JBL Pure Bass Sound.
      Connectivity: Upgraded to Bluetooth 5.2 with reliable Dual Connect (stereo or independent mono use).
      Fit System: Bendable Powerhook™ combined with TwistLock™ secure-fit stabilizers. Flexing the hook open automatically triggers the Bluetooth pairing sequence.
      Active Noise Cancelling: None. It relies strictly on tight, physical noise isolation.
