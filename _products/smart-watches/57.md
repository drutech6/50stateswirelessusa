  The Apple Watch Series 4 remains a landmark device in Apple's wearable timeline because it introduced the foundational screen layout, thinner case shape, and lifesaving health features that Apple still uses in its modern smartwatches. 
  🚀 Groundbreaking Features
  
      The Big Redesign: Series 4 expanded the screen view by shrinking the borders, providing a 30% larger display canvas than the older Series 3 while making the chassis notably thinner. 
      Medical-Grade ECG: It was the first consumer smartwatch to feature built-in titanium electrodes, allowing users to take an Electrocardiogram (ECG) directly from their wrist to detect irregular heart rhythms. 
      Fall Detection: Introduced high-strength motion sensors that can detect a heavy slip or trip, automatically dialing emergency services if you remain immobile for a full minute. 
      Haptic Feedback Crown: Re-engineered the side dial wheel to click with subtle vibration ticks, mimicking the feel of a mechanical luxury timepiece. 
  
  🎨 Case Architecture & Sizes
  The Series 4 moved away from the original small watch sizing to establish two modern, rounded-corner dimensions: 
  1. The 40mm Configuration
  
      Physical Dimensions: 40mm x 34mm x 10.7mm
      Weight: 30.1 grams (Aluminum Case) 
  
  2. The 44mm Configuration
  
      Physical Dimensions: 44mm x 38mm x 10.7mm
      Weight: 36.7 grams (Aluminum Case)
