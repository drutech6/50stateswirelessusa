  The Meta Quest Pro (256GB) is Meta's high-end headset designed for collaboration and professional work. While the Quest 3 has a more powerful processor, the Pro remains unique for its advanced social features like Face and Eye Tracking.
  Core Technical Specifications
  The Pro model uses premium hardware designed for comfort and multitasking:
  
      Performance & Power:
          Processor: Qualcomm Snapdragon XR2+ Gen 1 (optimized for better thermal management than the standard XR2).
          RAM: 12,000MB (12GB) LPDDR5, allowing for better multitasking and complex apps.
          Storage: 256GB internal storage.
      Display & Optics:
          Resolution: 1800 x 1920 pixels per eye.
          Lenses: Pancake lenses that offer 75% more contrast and a much larger "sweet spot" than traditional lenses.
          Field of View: 106° horizontal and 96° vertical.
          Local Dimming: Uses over 500 individual LED backlights to provide deeper blacks and richer colors.
      Advanced Tracking Features:
          Eye Tracking: Enables foveated rendering (focusing detail where you look) and natural eye contact in social apps.
          Face Tracking: Mimics your actual facial expressions on your avatar in real-time.
          Controllers: Quest Pro controllers feature independent tracking (using their own cameras) so they never lose position, even when behind your back.
      Mixed Reality & Connectivity:
          Passthrough: High-resolution, full-color mixed reality using specialized outward-facing cameras.
          Audio: Integrated spatial audio and dual 3.5mm audio jacks (one on each side).
          Wireless: Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 5.2.
      Physical Design:
          Weight: 722 grams (balanced by a rear-mounted battery for better ergonomics).
          Battery Life: Approximately 1.5 to 2 hours (typically used with the included charging dock).
