  The HP 15.6-inch Laptop with the Intel Processor N200 is built on a modern, energy-efficient architecture (Alder Lake-N) designed to handle daily productivity without a high power draw. 
  Performance Specifications
  
      Processor: Intel Processor N200 (Quad-core, up to 3.7 GHz burst). This chip is significantly more capable than older Celeron or Pentium Silver processors.
      Graphics: Integrated Intel UHD Graphics (supports 4K video playback).
      RAM:
          Windows Models: Typically 4GB DDR4-3200. Some models feature an open slot allowing an upgrade to 16GB.
          Chromebook Models: Typically 8GB LPDDR5-4800 (non-upgradeable).
      Storage:
          Windows Models: 128GB UFS (Universal Flash Storage), which is faster than traditional eMMC but slower than NVMe SSDs.
          Chromebook Models: 64GB eMMC. 
  
  Display & Multimedia
  
      Screen: 15.6-inch diagonal HD (1366 x 768) micro-edge, anti-glare, 250 nits brightness.
      Webcam: HP True Vision 720p HD camera with temporal noise reduction and a physical privacy shutter.
      Audio: Dual speakers with noise-canceling microphones. 
  
  Connectivity & Ports
  
      Wireless: Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) and Bluetooth 5.3.
      Physical Ports:
          1x USB Type-C (5Gbps signaling rate; supports data only).
          2x USB Type-A (5Gbps signaling rate).
          1x HDMI 1.4b.
          1x Headphone/microphone combo jack.
          1x AC smart pin. 
  
  Battery & Build
  
      Battery: 3-cell, 41 Wh Li-ion battery.
      Battery Life: Up to 11.5 hours for Windows; up to 10.5 hours for Chromebook.
      Fast Charge: Approximately 50% in 45 minutes.
      Weight: ~3.5 to 3.8 lbs (approx. 1.6–1.7 kg).
      Keyboard: Full-size with a numeric keypad. 
  
  Software
  
      Windows Models: Windows 11 Home in S mode (optimized for security). Often includes a 1-year Microsoft 365 Personal subscription.
      Chromebook Models: ChromeOS (includes Google Play Store support).
