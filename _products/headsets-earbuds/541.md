description: |
  Unlike the previous Bluetooth wireless options we discussed, the JBL Tune 110
  (also known as the JBL T110
  ) is a traditional wired entry-level headset. It bypasses wireless entirely, relying on a physical 3.5mm audio jack for zero-latency plug-and-play connectivity. 
  Key Technical Specifications
  
      Connectivity: Standard 3.5mm audio plug angled design. It requires a headphone jack or an external USB-C/Lightning dongle adapter to connect to modern smartphones.
      Audio Drivers: Uses 9 mm dynamic drivers engineered with JBL Pure Bass Sound. Expect a heavy, deep, and punchy low-end response.
      Cable Architecture: Outfitted with a 3.65-foot (1.11 meter) flat cable. The flat structural design acts as a physical deterrent to stop the wire from knotting up in your pocket.
      Control Remote: Features a 1-button in-line remote module containing a single physical button and a mono microphone.
      Battery & Power: None. Because it is a strictly wired device, it draws its minimal passive power directly from your playback source. It never needs to be recharged. 
  
  Inline Control Schemes
  Because it lacks a multi-button layout, the single inline clicker relies on tap sequences: 
  
      Single Click: Pause/Play track or Answer/End incoming phone calls.
      Double Click: Skip forward to the next musical track.
      Long Press: Activates your phone's native voice assistant (Google Assistant or Siri).
      Note: There are no physical volume adjustments on the cable; volume must be modified via your connected device
