  The JBL Flip 6
  is a portable Bluetooth speaker featuring a 2-way speaker system designed for powerful "JBL Original Pro Sound" with high clarity and deep bass. 
  Audio & Performance Specs
  
      Transducers: Features a 45 x 80mm racetrack-shaped woofer (1.75 x 3 inches) and a separate 16mm tweeter (0.6 inches).
      Output Power: Total of 30W RMS, split between 20W for the woofer and 10W for the tweeter.
      Frequency Response: 63 Hz – 20k Hz.
      Signal-to-Noise Ratio: >80 dB.
      Bass Radiators: Optimized dual passive radiators fine-tuned with Harman's advanced algorithm for deep low-end. 
  
  Battery & Connectivity
  
      Battery Life: Up to 12 hours of playtime (varies by volume and content).
      Charging: Uses a USB-C port with a 2.5-hour full recharge time (at 5V/3A).
      Bluetooth Version: 5.1 supporting A2DP 1.3 and AVRCP 1.6 profiles.
      PartyBoost: Allows you to pair two compatible speakers for stereo sound or link multiple speakers for a larger party experience. 
  
  Design & Build
  
      Durability: IP67 rated, meaning it is fully dustproof and waterproof (can be submerged in up to 1 meter of water for 30 minutes).
      Dimensions: Approximately 7.0 x 2.6 x 2.8 inches (17.8 x 6.8 x 7.2 cm).
      Weight: 1.21 lbs (0.55 kg).
      Charging Protection: Includes a USB-C charging protection alert that sounds if water, salt, or chemicals are detected in the connector. 
  
  According to the official JBL Flip 6
  Product Page, the device is compatible with the JBL Portable app, which offers firmware updates and a basic 3-band equalizer for custom sound tuning.
