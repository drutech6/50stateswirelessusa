description: |
  The original Apple AirPods Max (1st Gen) 2020
  represents the inaugural hardware design of Apple's flagship over-ear headphones. Originally launched in December 2020 for $549.00, this first-generation model has been discontinued by Apple but remains highly sought after on the secondary market as an affordable entryway into Apple’s premium audio system. 
  Key Technical Specifications
  
      Audio Processor: Powered by Dual Apple H1 Headphone Chips (one custom processor embedded inside each individual ear cup).
      Charging Port: Utilizes a baseline Lightning connector port located at the bottom of the right ear cup for power delivery.
      Battery Performance: Rated for up to 20 hours of continuous battery life with Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) or Spatial Audio enabled.
      Microphone Layout: Equipped with 9 total microphones; 8 microphones drive ANC and 3 handle clear voice pickup.
      Wireless Protocol: Connects via Bluetooth 5.0 wireless standard.
      Physical Weight: Weighs 13.6 ounces (386 grams) due to its anodized aluminum ear cups and stainless steel frame components. 
  
  Top Ecosystem Features
  
      Computational Audio: The dual H1 processors execute 10 audio cores to adapt real-time sound, adjust ambient noise cancellation, and block out outside noise.
      Personalized Spatial Audio: Tracks dynamic head movements via built-in gyroscopes and accelerometers to simulate a theater-like 3D surround sound experience.
      Apple Ecosystem Sync: Features seamless proximity pairing, automatic switching between an iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple TV, and shared audio streaming across multiple pairs.
      Tactile Hardware Controls: Uses a physical Digital Crown for volume tracking and track navigation, alongside a dedicated noise control button to cycle between ANC and Transparency mode. 
  
  Generational Differences
  
      Vs. 2024 Mid-Gen Refresh: The 2024 model replaced the legacy Lightning port with a universal USB-C port, modified the color scheme, and added native 24-bit lossless digital audio over a cable, but retained the exact same H1 processor and internal acoustic engine.
      Vs. 2026 AirPods Max 2: The AirPods Max 2 upgrades the core processor architecture to Dual H2 Chips. This hardware shift unlocks up to 1.5x more effective ANC, improved bass acoustics, and intelligent software features like Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, and Live Translation that are entirely missing on the 1st Gen 2020 version.
