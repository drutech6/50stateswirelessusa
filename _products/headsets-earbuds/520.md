  The Apple AirPods Pro (2nd Gen) with Wireless MagSafe Charging (Lightning) is the original version of Apple's flagship premium in-ear earbuds, featuring the classic proprietary Lightning port on the charging case.
  Key Specifications & Features
  
      Apple H2 Silicon: Powered by the H2 chip, driving up to twice the amount of Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) compared to the first-generation AirPods Pro, alongside a high-fidelity adaptive Transparency Mode.
      Intelligent Audio: Unlocks Adaptive Audio (which dynamically blends ANC and Transparency to match your changing environment) and Conversation Awareness (which automatically lowers your media volume when you start speaking).
      Silicone Ear Tips: Ships with four sizes of silicone ear tips (XS, S, M, L) to provide a tight, comfortable acoustic seal that blocks out ambient noise.
      Personalized Spatial Audio: Uses custom drivers and internal gyroscopes to map out a theater-like 3D surround sound field complete with dynamic head tracking.
      Hearing Health Features: Supports Apple's over-the-air software capabilities that unlock clinical-grade Hearing Aid functionality, a localized hearing test, and active loud noise hearing protection.
  
  Battery & Charging Logistics
  
      Playtime: Delivers up to 6 hours of continuous listening time on a single charge with ANC actively running.
      Total Reservoir: The MagSafe Charging Case provides up to 30 hours of total listening time.
      Fast Fuel: Tucking the earbuds into the case for 5 minutes yields roughly 1 hour of playback.
      Smart MagSafe Case: The charging case features a proprietary Lightning port. It can also be powered wirelessly using a MagSafe charger, an Apple Watch charging puck, or standard Qi wireless pads. The case includes an integrated speaker for precision Find My proximity tracking and a physical lanyard loop anchor.
  
  Crucial Hardware Considerations
  This specific iteration relies on a Lightning connector port rather than the universally adopted USB-C port found on the later 2023 refresh. Additionally, this original 2022 Lightning version does not support Lossless Audio when paired with the Apple Vision Pro, and it features an IPX4 sweatproof rating for the buds only (the newer USB-C case features an upgraded IP54 dust and water rating).
