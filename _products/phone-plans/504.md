  (Phone not Included) The AT&T 50GB Prepaid Plan is primarily offered as a Prepaid Data-Only Plan priced at $55 per month for tablets, mobile hotspots, and compatible routers, rather than standard cell phones. 
  However, depending on what type of device you have, AT&T structures its 50GB prepaid allotments across a few different options:
  1. Data-Only Plan (Tablets, Laptops, Hotspots) 
  
      Price: $55 per month.
      Data Limits: You get 50GB of 5G / 4G LTE high-speed data.
      Overages: If you run out of data, you can buy a 5GB add-on for $10.
      Availability: Accessible both online and in AT&T retail environments. 
  
  2. Standard Smartphone Plan (In-Store Only)     If you are looking for a standalone phone plan with this exact data bucket, AT&T lists an in-store exclusive phone plan offering 50GB of data for $55 per month. This includes traditional nationwide talk and text alongside your 5G network access.
