  The Apple iPad Pro 11-inch M4 Generation (Wi-Fi + Cellular, 256GB / 8GB RAM) builds on the core Wi-Fi model by adding persistent 5G mobile data connectivity and dedicated GPS hardware. 
  The complete technical specifications from the official Apple Support Tech Specs for this configuration include: 
  Cellular & Location Upgrades
  
      Cellular Networks: 5G connectivity (sub-6 GHz) with 4x4 MIMO; Gigabit LTE with 4x4 MIMO and LAA.
      SIM Compatibility: eSIM only format. (Note: This model does not have a physical nano-SIM slot; cellular service must be activated digitally).
      Satellite & Maps GPS: Dedicated GPS/GNSS hardware chip for accurate real-time navigation and mapping (exclusive to Cellular models). 
  
  Processor & Memory
  
      Chip: Apple M4 Chip.
      CPU: 9-core CPU with 3 performance cores and 6 efficiency cores.
      GPU: 10-core GPU featuring hardware-accelerated ray tracing and mesh shading.
      Neural Engine: 16-core Neural Engine built for Apple Intelligence workloads.
      Memory Bandwidth: 153GB/s.
      RAM: 8GB unified memory. 
  
  Display
  
      Screen Type: Ultra Retina XDR utilizing a dual-panel Tandem OLED system.
      Resolution: 2420-by-1668-pixel resolution at 264 ppi.
      Refresh Rate: ProMotion technology with adaptive refresh rates from 10Hz to 120Hz.
      Brightness: 1000 nits max full-screen SDR/XDR; 1600 nits peak HDR brightness.
      Glass Options: Standard glass layout only. (Nano-texture matte glass is restricted to 1TB/2TB models). 
  
  Camera, Video & Audio
  
      Rear Camera: 12MP Wide camera with ƒ/1.8 aperture, adaptive True Tone flash, and integrated LiDAR Scanner.
      Front Camera: Landscape-oriented 12MP Ultra Wide camera with ƒ/2.4 aperture, Center Stage tracking, and Face ID secure login.
      Video Recording: 4K video recording up to 60 fps; ProRes video recording up to 4K at 30 fps.
      Audio Output: Immersive four-speaker audio system paired with a studio-quality four-mic array. 
  
  Physical Architecture & Power
  
      Thickness: Exceptionally thin profile measuring 0.21 inch (5.3 mm).
      Width & Height: 6.99 inches (177.5 mm) wide by 9.83 inches (249.7 mm) high.
      Weight: Ultra-light 1.00 pound (452 grams) (weighs just 8 grams more than the Wi-Fi only model due to cellular antennas).
      Ports: One Thunderbolt / USB 4 port supporting charging, DisplayPort out, and up to 40Gb/s transfer speed.
      Battery Life: Built-in 31.29-watt-hour battery providing up to 10 hours of web surfing on Wi-Fi or up to 9 hours of web surfing using a cellular data network. 
  
  Accessory Support
  
      Apple Pencil: Fully compatible with the haptic-enabled Apple Pencil Pro and the standard Apple Pencil (USB-C).
      Keyboard: Native support for the aluminum palm-rest Magic Keyboard for iPad Pro (M4).
