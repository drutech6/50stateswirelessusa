  The iPad (10th Generation), released in 2022, serves as the modern entry-level iPad. It features a major design overhaul compared to the 9th Gen, moving to a larger, all-screen display and replacing the Lightning port with USB-C.

  Feature	Specification
  Display	10.9-inch Liquid Retina (2360 x 1640) at 264 ppi; 500 nits brightness
  Chipset	A14 Bionic (6-core CPU, 4-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine)
  Storage	64 GB or 256 GB
  Front Camera	Landscape 12 MP Ultra Wide; Center Stage support
  Rear Camera	12 MP Wide; 4K video recording at up to 60 fps
  Audio	Landscape stereo speakers; dual microphones
  Battery	Up to 10 hours (Wi-Fi) or 9 hours (Cellular)
  Authentication	Touch ID integrated into the top power button
  Connectivity	Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax); Optional 5G (sub-6 GHz); Bluetooth 5.2
  Dimensions	9.79 x 7.07 x 0.28 inches (248.6 x 179.5 x 7 mm)
  Weight	1.05 lbs (477 g) for Wi-Fi; 1.06 lbs (481 g) for Cellular
  Key Hardware Notes
  
      Pencil Support: It supports the Apple Pencil (USB-C) and Apple Pencil (1st Gen). Note that the 1st Gen Pencil requires a USB-C to Apple Pencil Adapter to charge and pair.
      Keyboard Support: Specifically designed to work with the Magic Keyboard Folio, which features a two-piece design and a full row of function keys.
      Color Options: Available in four bold finishes: Silver, Blue, Pink, and Yellow.
