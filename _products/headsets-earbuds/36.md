  The Motorola Moto XT500+ Wireless Over-Ear Headphones

  Core Upgrades & Specifications
  
      Modern USB-C Charging: Unlike the older micro-USB architecture used on the XT220, the XT500+
      updates to a faster, reversible USB Type-C port that fully tops off the battery in under 2 hours.
      25-Hour Battery Runtime: Extends the continuous wireless music playback window slightly up to 25 hours total (with 22 hours of continuous talk time).
      40mm Bass-Boost Drivers: Fitted with large 40mm dynamic drivers engineered with hard-coded factory acoustic tuning to emphasize deep bass responses and crisp vocal tracking.
      Dual-Mode 3.5mm Aux Support: Connects cord-free via Bluetooth 5.0, but packages a matching detachable 3.5mm auxiliary cord to transition into an unpowered wired layout when the battery runs out.
      Compact Foldable Footprint: Weighs only 190 grams and utilizes inward-swiveling hinges that let the earcups fold flat against the padded headband to take up minimal space in a backpack.
      Integrated Shell Microphone: A built-in microphone on the right earcup manages wireless phone call audio, and provides physical button short-cuts to summon native smartphone helper utilities like Siri or Google Assistant.
