  The Apple AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation (2024)
  represents a massive design milestone, introducing digital noise management to an open-ear design for the first time. Unlike the AirPods Pro series, these do not use silicone ear tips, making them ideal for users who dislike the clogged feeling of deep in-ear inserts. 
  Key Specifications & Features
  
      Advanced H2 Silicon: Powered by the premium Apple H2 chip, unlocking advanced computational audio features like Adaptive Audio, high-fidelity Transparency Mode, and Conversation Awareness (which drops volume when you begin speaking).
      Active Noise Cancellation (ANC): The specialized open-ear hardware geometry works with the H2 chip to actively mitigate low-frequency environmental rumble like airplane engines, city traffic, and office HVAC systems.
      Siri Gestures: Includes Siri Interactions, allowing you to silently nod your head "yes" or shake your head "no" to accept, mute, or decline incoming calls and notifications hands-free.
      Crystal Clear Calls: Uses Voice Isolation and dual beamforming microphones to isolate your voice while stripping out harsh ambient background noise during phone calls.
      Audio & Fit Redesign: Built with an updated, ergonomically mapped acoustic architecture for greater stability and a shorter force-sensor stem. It also supports dynamic Personalized Spatial Audio with head tracking.
      IP54 Durability: Both the individual earbuds and the accompanying case are rated IP54 dust, sweat, and water-resistant, making them safe for rigorous gym workouts or light rain. 
  
  Battery & Charging Logistics
  
      Playtime: Delivers up to 4 hours of playback on a single charge with ANC active (up to 5 hours with ANC toggled off).
      Total Reservoir: The case provides up to 20 total hours of battery life with ANC (and up to 30 hours without ANC).
      Fast Fuel: Dropping the earbuds into the case for just 5 minutes yields roughly 1 hour of continuous listening.
      Upgraded Wireless Case: The charging case is Apple's smallest to date. It features a built-in acoustic speaker for precise Apple Find My tracking and charges via USB-C, an Apple Watch charger, or any standard Qi wireless charging pad.
