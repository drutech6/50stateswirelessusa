  Apple AirPods Max (USB-C):
  Audio Technology & Hardware
  
      Drivers: 40mm Apple-designed dynamic transducer in each ear cup for low distortion.
      Processors: Dual Apple H1 headphone chips (one in each ear cup) utilizing 10 audio cores.
      Microphones: 9 total microphones. 8 handle Active Noise Cancellation (ANC); 3 handle voice pickup (2 shared with ANC, 1 dedicated).
      Sensors: Optical, position, case-detect, and accelerometer sensors in each ear cup, plus a gyroscope in the left ear cup.
  
  Connectivity & Audio Formats
  
      Bluetooth: Version 5.0 wireless protocol.
      Physical Port: USB-C for charging and audio.
      Wired Audio Support: Supports native 24-bit/48kHz lossless digital audio input via direct USB-C connection.
      Bluetooth Codecs: AAC and SBC.
  
  Battery & Power
  
      Playback Time: Up to 20 hours with ANC, Transparency mode, or Spatial Audio enabled.
      Fast Charge: A 5-minute charge over USB-C yields approximately 1.5 hours of playback time.
      Standby Mode: Automatically drops into an ultra-low-power standby mode when placed inside the Smart Case.
  
  Design, Materials & Dimensions
  
      Ear Cups: Anodized aluminum with independent pivoting hinges to balance clamping pressure.
      Ear Cushions: Acoustically engineered memory foam wrapped in a breathable knit textile fabric (magnetically detachable).
      Headband: Stainless steel frame wrapped in a flexible, breathable knit-mesh canopy.
      Weight: 13.6 ounces (386 grams).
      Dimensions: 6.64 x 3.28 x 7.37 inches (168.6 x 83.4 x 187.3 mm).
