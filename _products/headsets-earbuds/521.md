  The Apple AirPods (3rd Gen)
  , originally introduced in late 2021, brought a major design overhaul to Apple’s standard open-ear line by borrowing the shorter-stem silhouette of the AirPods Pro. Unlike the Pro series, they do not have silicone ear tips or Active Noise Cancellation (ANC). Instead, they focus on open-ear comfort and a naturally spacious acoustic signature. 
  Key Specifications & Features
  
      Personalized Spatial Audio: Features built-in gyroscopes and accelerometers to deliver immersive 3D spatial audio with dynamic head tracking, centering theater-like sound fields around you when streaming compatible Dolby Atmos movies or tracks.
      H1 Headphone Chip: Powered by Apple's classic H1 processor, unlocking seamless one-touch proximity pairing, automatic shifting between your iCloud-linked devices, and hands-free "Hey Siri" execution.
      Sweat & Water Defense: Both individual earbuds and the accompanying charging case carry an IPX4 durability rating, making them fully certified to survive heavy workouts, runs, and unexpected rain showers.
      Advanced Skin-Detect Sensor: Equipped with an accurate proximity sensor that tracks contact with human skin. This stops audio from accidentally playing when the buds are sliding around inside your pocket or sitting loose on a desk.
      Tactile Force Control: Replaces the old "tapping" mechanics of previous generations with a physical pressure force sensor integrated directly into the shorter stems. You squeeze the stem once to play/pause and twice or three times to skip tracks. 
  
  Battery & Power Logistics 
  
      Playtime: Delivers up to 6 hours of continuous listening on a single full charge.
      Total Reservoir: The matching companion case supplies up to an extra 24 hours of juice, pushing total mobile runtime to 30 hours.
      Fast Fuel: Dropping the earbuds inside a dead case for exactly 5 minutes stores enough fast backup power for 1 hour of playback. 
  
  Charging Case Variants
  This 2021 generation was sold across two distinct case hardware setups: 
  
      Lightning Charging Case: Powering occurs strictly via standard wired physical Lightning cable infrastructure.
      MagSafe Charging Case: Supports wired tracking but also nests magnetically onto MagSafe wireless magnets, standard Qi wireless charging mats, or an Apple Watch charger puck.
