  Core Technical Upgrades & Hardware
  
      Adaptive Noise Cancellation (ANC): The internal acoustic microphone array constantly measures surrounding environments to systematically isolate subways, office chatter, and street noise.
      Ambient Aware & TalkThru: Lets you instantly loop outside environment noise back through the speaker drivers for sidewalk walking safety, or drops media volume down to let you hold a conversation without removing the headset.
      70-Hour Max Battery: Delivers an astonishing 70 hours of consecutive wireless runtime with ANC deactivated. Enabling Adaptive ANC still leaves you with a highly impressive 44 hours of playtime on a single charge.
      Lightning-Fast Speed Charging: Hooking the low battery chassis to a Type-C cable for just 5 minutes yields 3 full hours of instant wireless audio playback.
      Bluetooth 5.3 & LE Audio: Utilizes the latest energy-efficient wireless protocol for lower latency, faster syncing, and seamless Multi-Device Multipoint shifting between your laptop and smartphone.
      JBL Headphones App Synergy: Pairs natively with the official software suite to manually balance the graphical EQ bands, tweak automated standby timers, or toggle between prioritized "Audio" and "Video" low-latency modes. 
  
  Upgrading from the Tune 520BT
  If you are evaluating these against the step-down JBL Tune 520BT
  headphones you checked out earlier, the Tune 670NC
  provides distinct advantages:
  
      Active Isolation: The Tune 520BT
      uses physical clamping pressure alone to muffle noise. The Tune 670NC
      uses motorized microphones to digitally cancel outside distractions.
      Wired Fallback Option: While the Tune 520BT
      is strictly wireless, the Tune 670NC
      features a detachable auxiliary cable port so you can switch to an unpowered wired connection if your battery runs out.
      Voice Tracking: Uses advanced VoiceAware integration so you can select exactly how much of your own voice echoes back through the speakers during professional cell phone calls.
