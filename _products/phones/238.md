Display: A 6.5-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display with 120Hz ProMotion and Always-On functionality. It reaches a peak outdoor brightness of 3,000 nits. Connectivity: Equipped with Apple's custom C1X modem and N1 networking chip, supporting Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0. The device is eSIM only worldwide.
