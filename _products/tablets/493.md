  The exact technical specifications for the Apple iPad Pro 11-inch M4 Generation (Wi-Fi + Cellular, 1TB Storage / 16GB RAM) from the official Apple documentation include:
  1. Cellular Connectivity & Standalone GPS
  
      Mobile Network Bandwidth: Built-in 5G connectivity (sub-6 GHz and mmWave) with 4x4 MIMO and Gigabit LTE.
      SIM Architecture: Uses eSIM-only activation [JJGCQ37HLR]. There is no physical nano-SIM card tray.
      Location Tracking: Dedicated, standalone GPS/GNSS hardware chip for precise real-time offline mapping and navigation (exclusive to Cellular models). 
  
  2. Compute Performance & Unified RAM
  
      Fully Enabled Processor: Features the un-binned version of the Apple M4 chip (built on a 3-nanometer process).
      CPU Layout: 10-core CPU containing 4 performance cores and 6 efficiency cores (lower storage models are limited to a 9-core CPU).
      GPU Layout: 10-core GPU featuring hardware-accelerated ray tracing, mesh shading, and Dynamic Caching.
      System RAM: 16GB of Unified Memory with an expansive memory bandwidth speed of 153GB/s (doubled from the 8GB RAM on smaller capacities).
      Artificial Intelligence Engine: 16-core Neural Engine capable of 38 trillion operations per second to drive local Apple Intelligence workflows. 
  
  3. Ultra Retina XDR Display 
  
      Display Panel: Ultra Retina XDR utilizing a groundbreaking Tandem OLED dual-panel design.
      Spatial Resolution: 2420-by-1668-pixel resolution at a dense 264 pixels per inch (ppi).
      Motion Dynamics: ProMotion technology facilitating adaptive, battery-saving refresh rates from 10Hz to 120Hz.
      Luminance Capabilities: 1000 nits maximum full-screen brightness across SDR and XDR content; 1600 nits peak brightness for HDR formats.
      Contrast Performance: True-to-life 2,000,000:1 contrast ratio with deep, pure ink blacks.
      Glass Finishing Options: Available with standard reflection-reducing glass or specialized Nano-texture display glass that scatters ambient glare. 
  
  4. Cameras, Capture, and Audio
  
      Rear Array: 12MP Wide camera (
      aperture) with an Adaptive True Tone flash and an integrated LiDAR Scanner for spatial depth and AR.
      Front Array: Landscape-oriented 12MP Ultra Wide camera (
      aperture) with Center Stage tracking and secure Face ID login.
      Video Codecs: Captures up to 4K at 60 fps; supports hardware-accelerated ProRes video encoding up to 4K at 30 fps.
      Sound Systems: Immersive four-speaker audio matched with a studio-grade four-microphone array. 
  
  5. Mechanical Chassis & Power Delivery
  
      Form Factor: Exceptionally slim profile measuring just 0.21 inch (5.3 mm).
      Hardware Weight: Extremely light desktop substitute scaling in at 1.00 pound (452 grams).
      I/O Routing: Universal Thunderbolt / USB 4 port driving power delivery, high-speed storage, and external monitors up to 6K resolution.
      Battery Lifespan: Built-in 31.29-watt-hour battery. Offers up to 10 hours of web surfing on Wi-Fi or up to 9 hours of web surfing using a mobile cellular data network. 
  
  6. Accessory Ecosystem
  
      Apple Pencil: Fully compatible with the haptic-enabled Apple Pencil Pro and the standard Apple Pencil (USB-C).
      Keyboard Docking: Designed to mate seamlessly with the aluminum palm-rest Magic Keyboard for iPad Pro (M4).
