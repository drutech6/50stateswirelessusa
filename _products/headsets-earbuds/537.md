  Key Technical Specifications
  
      Secure Powerhook Design: Features an ergonomic, bendable ear hook that wraps entirely around your ear. The hook acts as the power switch—flexing it open automatically turns the earbud on and enters pairing mode.
      IPX7 Waterproofing: Built to withstand full immersion in up to 1 meter of water for 30 minutes. They are fully sweatproof and can be easily rinsed off after a hard workout.
      Audio Drivers: Equipped with larger 10mm dynamic drivers tuned with JBL Pure Bass Sound to provide an energetic, thumping beat profile meant to fuel exercise.
      Battery Life: Delivers up to 30 hours of total playback. The buds provide 6 hours of continuous playtime, complemented by an extra 24 hours from the charging case.
      Dual Connect & Mono Use: Either earbud can be used independently for music or stereo calls, allowing you to keep one ear completely open to your surroundings. 
  
  Notable Limitations
  
      No Active Noise Cancelling (ANC): These buds rely entirely on tight passive noise isolation from the silicone tips.
      No Ambient Pass-Through Mode: There is no software transparency option. To safely hear traffic or talk to a gym partner, you must physically unhook and remove an earbud.
      No App Support: Unlike the Tour Pro series, the Endurance Peak II
      does not connect to the JBL Headphones app. You cannot adjust the factory equalizer or customize the swipe-to-volume touch controls through official software.
