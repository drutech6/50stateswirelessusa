  The Apple AirPods Pro (1st Gen)
  , released on October 30, 2019, marked Apple's entry into premium, in-ear active noise-canceling headphones. Powered by the Apple H1 chip, they introduced a customizable fit with silicone ear tips and seamless ecosystem integration. 
  Key Features & Specifications
  
      Audio Technology: Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), Transparency mode, Adaptive EQ, and Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking.
      Controls: Stem-based force sensors instead of double-taps, allowing users to switch modes, control playback, and summon Siri.
      Battery Life: Up to 4.5 hours of listening time on a single charge (5 hours with ANC/Transparency off) and over 24 hours with the charging case.
      Charging: The original 2019 model shipped with a standard Wireless Charging Case that charges via Lightning or Qi-certified mats (Note: Apple refreshed this generation in 2021 specifically to add a magnetic MagSafe charging case).
      Durability: IPX4 sweat and water resistance for the earbuds only (the case is not resistant).
