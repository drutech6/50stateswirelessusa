  The JBL Grip
  (released in late 2025) is an ultra-portable Bluetooth speaker designed with a "seltzer can" shape for easy handling. It sits between the JBL Go 4 and the Flip series, offering a more rugged build and built-in mood lighting. 
  Key Specifications
  
      Audio Performance: Features a 16W RMS racetrack-shaped full-range driver (43 x 80 mm) and dual passive radiators. It uses AI Sound Boost to optimize audio in real time and reduce distortion at high volumes.
      Durability: Rated IP68, making it fully dustproof and waterproof (submersible up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes). It is also "drop-proof," tested to survive a 1-meter fall onto concrete.
      Battery Life: Delivers 12 hours of standard playtime, which can be extended to 14 hours using the "Playtime Boost" feature. A full recharge takes approximately 3 hours via USB-C.
      Connectivity: Equipped with Bluetooth 5.4 and supports Auracast™ for stereo pairing or connecting an unlimited number of compatible JBL speakers.
      Unique Features: Includes a customizable ambient light strip on the back panel and an integrated utility loop for clipping to bags or gear. 
  
  Physical Design
  
      Dimensions: Approximately 6 x 2.5 x 2.6 inches (15.3 x 6.4 x 6.5 cm).
      Weight: Extremely lightweight at 0.849 lbs (385 grams).
      Materials: Constructed using post-consumer recycled plastic and fabric with sustainable FSC-certified packaging.
