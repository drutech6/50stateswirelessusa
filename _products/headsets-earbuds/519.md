  The Apple AirPods Pro (2nd Gen) with Wireless MagSafe Charging (USB-C)
  is Apple's flagship premium in-ear earbud model. While the original 2nd generation debuted in 2022 with a Lightning port, Apple refreshed this exact model to feature a USB-C port, upgraded IP54 dust resistance, and support for Lossless Audio when paired with the Apple Vision Pro.
  Key Specifications & Features
  
      Apple H2 Silicon: Powered by the advanced H2 chip, driving twice the amount of Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) compared to the original AirPods Pro, alongside an adaptive Transparency Mode.
      Intelligent Audio Processing: Features Adaptive Audio, which dynamically blends Transparency mode and ANC to match the noise levels of your changing environment, and Conversation Awareness, which automatically lowers your media volume when you start speaking.
      Silicone Ear Tips: Includes four pairs of silicone ear tips (XS, S, M, L) to provide a tight, acoustic seal that creates deep passive isolation to support the digital ANC.
      Personalized Spatial Audio: Uses custom dynamic drivers and internal gyroscopes to map out an immersive 3D surround sound field complete with dynamic head tracking.
      Hearing Health Features: Equipped with advanced over-the-air software capabilities that unlock clinical-grade Hearing Aid functionality, a localized hearing test, and active loud noise hearing protection.
  
  Battery & Charging Logistics
  
      Playtime: Delivers up to 6 hours of continuous listening time on a single charge with ANC actively running.
      Total Reservoir: The MagSafe Charging Case provides up to 30 hours of total listening time.
      Fast Fuel: Tucking the earbuds into the case for 5 minutes yields roughly 1 hour of playback.
      Pro-Tier Smart Case: The refreshed case charges via USB-C, a MagSafe charger, an Apple Watch puck, or standard Qi wireless pads. It includes an integrated speaker for precision Find My proximity tracking and a lanyard loop anchor.
