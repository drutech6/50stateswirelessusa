  (Phone not Included) The Cricket Wireless Sensible™ 10GB Plan costs $30 per month with Auto Pay ($35 for your first month or if you pay manually). It provides exactly 10GB of high-speed data on AT&T’s nationwide network. 
  Key Plan Details
  
      Data Refresh: You get 10GB of high-speed data per month. If you exceed this cap, speeds are throttled to a slow 128 Kbps for the rest of your billing cycle.
      Network & Quality: Runs on AT&T's 5G network. Video streaming is capped at Standard Definition (480p).
      Line Consistency: If you have multiple lines, all lines must be on the Sensible™ 10GB Plan tier; you cannot mix and match it with unlimited family plans. 
  
  Dropped Features & Restrictions
  
      No Hotspot: Mobile hotspot capabilities are completely unavailable, and you cannot buy it as an add-on for this plan.
      No International Use: This plan strips away Cricket's usual international perks. It includes no roaming or calling/texting to or from Mexico and Canada, and no international texting.
      No Premium Extras: Cloud storage and Max (HBO Max) streaming are not included.
