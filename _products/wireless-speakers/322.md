  The Skullcandy Ounce+
  is an ultra-portable, weather-resistant mini speaker released as an upgraded version of the standard Ounce. The primary "plus" feature is the wireless linking system, which allows you to sync two Ounce+ speakers for a true stereo sound experience. 
  Key Specifications
  
      Audio Output: 20W peak power (5W RMS) with a 1.75-inch full-range driver and a downward-firing passive radiator for surprisingly deep bass for its size.
      Battery Life: Up to 16 hours on a single charge.
      Durability: IPX7 waterproof rating (can be submerged in up to 1 meter of water for 30 minutes) and impact-resistant construction.
      Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.3 with a range of up to 33 feet (10 meters).
      Charging: Modern USB-C charging port. 
  
  Main Features
  
      Ultra-Portable Design: Weighing only ~184g, it includes a built-in ballistic nylon carry strap to easily attach to backpacks, bikes, or golf bags.
      True Wireless Stereo (TWS): Connect two Ounce+ speakers wirelessly to play music in full stereo.
      Simple Controls: Features classic physical buttons for power, volume adjustment, and track control.
