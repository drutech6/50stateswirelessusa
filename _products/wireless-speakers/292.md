  The JBL PartyBox Encore Essential
  is a compact, cubic party speaker that delivers 100W RMS of JBL Original Pro Sound. It is designed for maximum portability with an integrated "grab-and-go" handle and an IPX4 splashproof rating, making it suitable for pool parties or beach outings. 
  Key Specifications
  
      Audio Performance: Features one 5.25" woofer and two 1.75" tweeters to provide deep bass and clear highs.
      Battery Life: Provides up to 6 hours of playtime, though real-world use at high volumes with lights active may reduce this to approximately 5 hours.
      Connectivity: Supports Bluetooth 5.1, USB playback, and a 3.5mm Aux input.
      Karaoke Ready: Includes a 1/4" wired microphone input with dedicated volume controls. 
  
  Core Features
  
      Dynamic Light Show: A built-in RGB ring light around the woofer and strobes synchronize to the music beat, customizable via the JBL PartyBox App.
      True Wireless Stereo (TWS): Allows you to wirelessly pair two Encore Essential speakers for a wider stereo soundstage.
      Bass Boost: Offers "Deep" and "Punchy" presets to enhance low-frequency impact depending on the environment. 
  
  Comparison: Encore Essential vs. PartyBox Encore
  While they share the same 100W sound architecture, the standard JBL PartyBox Encore
  (non-Essential) typically includes: 
  
      Longer Battery: Up to 10 hours of playtime.
      Wireless Mics: Usually bundled with two digital wireless microphones.
      Enhanced Controls: Physical knobs on the top panel for microphone echo, bass, and treble adjustments.
