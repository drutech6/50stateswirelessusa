  The Beats Powerbeats Pro (1st generation)

  Core Technical Specifications
  
      Audio Driver: 12 mm dynamic driver with a linear piston for reduced harmonic distortion.
      Connectivity: Class 1 Bluetooth 5.0 with up to 100m range and NFC support.
      Battery Life: Up to 9 hours of listening per bud and over 24 hours total with the charging case.
      Fast Fuel Charging: A 5-minute charge provides 1.5 hours of playback; a 15-minute charge provides 4.5 hours.
      Water Resistance: IPX4 rating, making them sweat and water-resistant.
      Sensors: Dual optical sensors for Auto-Play/Pause and motion accelerometers for power-saving sleep mode. 
  
  Design and Controls
  
      Fit: Includes adjustable, secure-fit earhooks and silicone ear tips in four different sizes.
      On-Ear Controls: Each earbud has an identical set of controls: a physical volume rocker and a multi-function "b" button for calls and music.
      Microphones: Dual beam-forming microphones in each earbud with a speech-detecting accelerometer to isolate your voice.
      Charging: The case uses a Lightning connector and does not support wireless charging.
