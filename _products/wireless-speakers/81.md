  The JBL Flip 7
  is a rugged, portable Bluetooth speaker that launched in 2025 as an upgrade to the popular Flip 6. It introduced several next-gen features, including AI Sound Boost for cleaner audio at high volumes, a tougher IP68 durability rating, and the new Auracast™ wireless standard. 
  Key Specifications & Performance
  
      Audio Output: Delivers 35W RMS total power, consisting of a 25W woofer and a 10W tweeter.
      AI Sound Boost: Uses real-time algorithms to maximize acoustic performance and reduce distortion during heavy playback.
      Battery Life: Offers up to 14 hours of standard playtime, extendable to 16 hours via the "Playtime Boost" feature (which conserves energy by reducing bass).
      Durability: Features an IP68 rating, making it fully dustproof and waterproof (submersible up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes). It is also certifiably drop-proof from heights up to 1 meter.
      Connectivity: Uses Bluetooth 5.4 for stable connections. It supports lossless audio playback when connected via its USB-C port to a compatible source.
      New Design Features: Replaces the standard carry loop with a PushLock system, allowing you to easily swap between a fabric loop and a carabiner (both included).
