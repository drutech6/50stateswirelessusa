  The JBL Vibe Beam 2

  , the primary difference is their physical design—the Vibe Beam 2

  Key Specifications
  
      Audio Design: Built with 8mm dynamic drivers fine-tuned for heavy JBL Pure Bass Sound.
      Noise Filtering: Features Active Noise Cancelling to block ambient sound, plus Ambient Aware and TalkThru modes for quick external communication.
      Playtime & Charging: Up to 40 total hours of battery life (10 hours in the stems + 30 hours in the case with ANC turned off). A quick 10-minute USB-C speed charge yields 3 full hours of playback.
      Call Performance: Upgraded to a 4-microphone system (2 per earbud) to isolate your voice from windy or loud environments.
      Resilience: The earbuds carry an IP54 rating (dust and water resistance), making them durable enough for intense gym workouts or light rain. The case holds an IPX2 rating.
      Connectivity: Utilizes Bluetooth 5.3 with Multi-Point Connection to transition between a phone and a laptop simultaneously. It also supports Google Fast Pair and Microsoft Swift Pair.
      JBL App Control: Fully compatible with the JBL Headphones App, allowing you to adjust equalizers (EQ), alter touch gestures, and turn on a Relax Mode loaded with peaceful ambient sounds.
