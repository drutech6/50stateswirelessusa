  The JBL Endurance Race TWS
  is an intermediate sport model that combines the rugged nature of the Endurance Peak line with the standard in-ear form factor of the Tour Pro series. It drops the physical over-ear hook design in favor of a low-profile Twistlock™ ear-wing enhancer. 
  Key Technical Specifications
  
      IP67 Dust and Waterproofing: Unlike the Endurance Peak 2 (IPX7), the Endurance Race
      adds an explicit dustproof certification. They are entirely dust-tight and can survive immersion in up to 1 meter of fresh water for 30 minutes.
      Fit Architecture: Uses an ergonomic silicone wing enhancer with Twistlock™ technology. You insert the earbud and twist it backward to lock it securely into the ear bowl.
      Audio Drivers: Uses compact 6mm dynamic drivers tuned to JBL Pure Bass Sound for a heavy low-end audio thump.
      Battery Performance: Offers up to 30 hours of total playback. The earbuds hold a lengthy 10 hours of continuous juice per charge, with an additional 20 hours housed inside the USB-C case.
      Connectivity: Employs Bluetooth 5.2 for a highly stable connection during movement.
      Smart Ambient & App Control: Unlike the Endurance Peak 2, these do connect to the official JBL Headphones App for custom EQ maps. It also features Ambient Aware and TalkThru transparency modes.
