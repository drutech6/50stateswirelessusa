  The JBL Partybox 110
  is a high-performance portable party speaker known for its powerful 160W sound output and a dynamic, customizable light show that synchronizes to your music's beat. It is designed for both indoor and outdoor use, featuring an IPX4 splashproof rating that protects it from light water exposure near pools or beaches. While it is substantial at approximately 23 lbs, it remains portable via built-in handles. 
  Key Specifications
  
      Audio Performance: Delivers 160W RMS of JBL Original Pro Sound with two 5.25" woofers and two 2.25" tweeters.
      Battery Life: Offers up to 12 hours of playtime, though realistic playback at higher volumes or with lights enabled is typically around 6 to 8 hours.
      Charging: Requires approximately 3.5 hours for a full charge via the included AC power cord.
      Inputs: Includes dedicated microphone and guitar inputs with individual volume controls, making it suitable for karaoke and live performances. 
  
  Feature Highlights
  
      JBL PartyBox App: Allows you to wirelessly control music playback, customize light patterns (such as strobes and colors), and update firmware.
      Bass Boost: Features two levels of adjustable deep bass to enhance low-frequency performance.
      Stereo Pairing: You can wirelessly pair two Partybox 110 speakers using True Wireless Stereo (TWS) technology or connect them via an aux cable for a larger soundstage.
      Versatile Playback: Supports streaming via Bluetooth 5.1, USB drives, or a standard Aux cable. 
  
  Expert & User Insights
  
      Sound Quality: Reviewers from AVForums and PopSci note that it maintains a clear sound profile even at high volumes, though some compression may occur at maximum output.
      Portability vs. Power: Users often compare it to the more powerful PartyBox 310 or 710, noting that the 110 is a "sweet spot" for small to medium gatherings of about 10–20 people.
      Sturdiness: While generally sturdy, the plastic body can be susceptible to scratches if dropped, though the front is protected by a metal grille.
